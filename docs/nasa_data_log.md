# NASA Open Data Integration Log

## 1. DONKI (Space Weather Database Notification, Knowledge, Information)

* **Dataset Purpose:** Real-time space weather monitoring (Solar Flares, Coronal Mass Ejections, Geomagnetic Storms).
* **In-Game Use Case:** 
  * Game mein intermittent Solar Radiation Alerts trigger honge.
  * Solar Flare event ke waqt player ko **Radiation Shielding** ko 100% active karna padega.
* **Educational Trade-Off:** 
  * Shielding power consume karti hai. Solar storm se bachne ke liye player ko power rationing karni padegi, jo young players ko severe space weather risk aur resource trade-off sikhata hai.

---

## 2. OSDR (Open Science Data Repository)

* **Dataset Purpose:** Astronaut biological research, microgravity health impacts, and radiation dosimetry findings.
* **In-Game Use Case:**
  * Jab Crew Health ya Morale drop hoga, tab UI par OSDR research-based educational info cards popup honge.
  * Example: Microgravity bone density loss, radiation exposure stress, aur sleep cycle disruption.
* **Educational Impact:**
  * Players sirf numbers nahi dekh rahe honge; wo samjhenge ki space environment real human biology ko kaise affect karta hai.

---

## 3. Artemis & Mars Surface Missions Data

* **Dataset Purpose:** Surface temperature swings, day/night solar generation variance, aur environmental hazards.
* **In-Game Use Case:**
  * Lunar night ya Martian dust storm events setup karne ke liye surface data conditions apply hongi, jisse solar power generation drop hogi.
